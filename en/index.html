(01) The idea

In the evening, the child waits while we cook. With Popote Duo, they get a real mission: cracking the egg, weighing, mixing. And dinner gets made together.

(02) The app

Three steps, one recipe

  1. Step 01 / 03

    The child picks a mission

    Each mission teaches one skill. The child picks a recipe out of three.

  2. Step 02 / 03

    Taking turns

    The screen says who does what, and Gari reads every instruction out loud.

  3. Step 03 / 03

    The skills book fills up

    Every skill the parent signs off earns a badge.

(04) Safety

Built for careful parents

You stay in control of everything.

  • A parent codeSettings and signing off skills sit behind a code your child doesn’t know.
  • Checked recipesEvery recipe is checked for age and safety.
  • Tastes, not health dataThe profile remembers what your child likes. No allergies are stored: check the allergens.
  • Your data, your choiceExport and delete from the settings. No ads, no tracking on the child’s side, data hosted in the EU.

(06) Your questions

Before you head to the kitchen

Another question? Email us at contact.popoteduo@gmail.com.

When does the app come out?

It’s already open, in your browser: create your account.

How much does Popote Duo cost?

Free: 1 recipe a month, out of 6. Premium costs €4.99 a month or €49.99 a year, first week free. For now, everything is free.

From what age?

From 4 to 10. The youngest get missions with no blade and no flame.

Will my child handle knives or the oven?

No. Anything sharp or hot is for the grown-up, and the screen says so at every step.

Do I need to know how to cook?

No. Simple recipes, guided step by step, out loud.

Which devices?

It’s a web app: it opens in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install.

What do you do with my data?

Only what your account needs. Nothing is ever sold, and you can export or delete everything from the app.
