Cook together, one task each

Popote Duo splits the screen in two: one task for the child, one for the parent. You cook together, each in your own role, from age 4.

Join the beta See the demo

How it works

Skills sized for small hands

A “no knives” filter

From age 4, recipes and tasks with no blades and no flames. Trickier skills come with age.

A skills book

Weighing, cracking an egg, whisking: every skill the parent signs off goes into the book and earns a badge.

Each their own task, on the same screen

At every step, the child gets a task their size while the parent handles the oven, the hob and the knife. Try it with the giant cookie: go through the steps and listen to Pépin.

Skills learned: weighing, mixing, folding in, setting the table.

Built for careful parents

A parent code, checked recipes, no health data: you stay in control of everything.

  • A parent codeSettings and signing off skills sit behind a code your child doesn’t know.
  • Checked recipesEvery recipe is checked for age and safety before it goes into the app.
  • Tastes, not health dataThe profile remembers the foods your child likes, tries or refuses. No allergies are stored: check the allergens in each recipe.
  • Your data, your choiceExport and delete it from the settings. No ads and no tracking on the child’s side, and data hosted in the European Union.
