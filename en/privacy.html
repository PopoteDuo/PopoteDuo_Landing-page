Privacy policy

This page covers the Popote Duo presentation website and its waiting list. The app will have its own policy, available before you sign up.

In short

  • We only collect what you type into the waiting list form.
  • No data about your child: only their age range, if you tick it.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no embedded social networks.
  • Your sign-up arrives by email in the team’s inbox. It is never sold or shared.
  • You can have it deleted at any time, just by sending us a message.

Who is responsible for your data

Popote Duo is a student project run by a team of five. The data controller is [to fill in: name of the person or school responsible].

For any question about your data: contact.popoteduo@gmail.com.

The website asks you for nothing unless you fill in the waiting list form. If you do, this information is sent to us by email:

Legal basis: your consent, given when you send the form (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it: just reply to one of our emails or write to us to be removed from the list.

Visitor statistics, without cookies

To know whether the website is useful, we count visits: number of visitors, pages viewed, country, type of device and the website you came from. These statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics:

  • no cookie or other tracker is stored on your device;
  • you are recognised for one day by a fingerprint computed from the request, which is then deleted: you cannot be followed from one day to the next or from one website to another;
  • we only see overall figures, never combined with other data;
  • statistics are kept for a limited time, less than 25 months.

This matches the conditions set by the CNIL, the French data protection authority, for audience measurement that does not require consent. That is why the website shows no cookie banner.

Who can access your data

Only the members of the Popote Duo team who manage the waiting list can access it. We never sell or rent your data. Our providers process it only on our behalf:

ProviderRoleWhere
VercelWebsite hosting, sending the form (from Paris) and visitor statisticsGlobal network, US company; transfer covered by the Data Privacy Framework

Fonts, photos, 3D and Pépin’s voice are served from our own website: your browser contacts no other service (no Google Fonts, no social networks).

How long we keep it

  • Waiting list: sign-up emails are kept until the app’s public launch, then for 12 months at most. They are deleted sooner if you unsubscribe or ask us to.
  • Visitor statistics: less than 25 months, with no personal data.
  • Host’s technical logs (IP address, date, page requested): a few days, for the website’s security.

Your rights

At any time, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use or restrict it. Write to us at the address above: we reply within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL: cnil.fr/en/complaints, or with the data protection authority of your country.

Security

The website keeps no sign-ups: it forwards them by email to the team’s inbox, which is protected by two-factor authentication. The form only accepts submissions from the website itself, and the number of submissions per person is limited. Traffic with the website is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date. For any significant change, we let the people on the list know by email.
